# RAFIKI Pin: target audience and content

Play Console › Policy › App content › Target audience and content.

- **Target age groups:** **18 and over** only. Do not tick any younger group.
- **Why:** RAFIKI Pin is a work tool for RAFIKI Outreach staff and volunteers (adults), given access by the office,
  and for adult donors the office invites. There is no public sign-up.
- **Could the app unintentionally appeal to children?** No. Answer "No" to "Appeals to children": the app is a
  field-reporting tool behind a sign-in, with no games, characters for children or child-directed content. (The
  friendly water-drop mascot on the welcome screen is for staff.)
- **Store presence:** the listing shouldn't use child-directed language or imagery (it doesn't).
- **Families policy:** not applicable (no children in the audience).
