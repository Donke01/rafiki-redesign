## English (en-US)

**App name** (10 of 30 characters)

    RAFIKI Pin

**Short description** (75 of 80 characters)

    The field app for RAFIKI Outreach: pin wells, report visits, track repairs.

**Full description** (about 1,700 of 4,000 characters)

    RAFIKI Pin is the field app of RAFIKI Outreach, a charity that drills wells and builds schools, clinics and farms with communities in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Malawi.

    It is a work tool for RAFIKI Outreach staff and volunteers. You need a sign-in from the office (a personal PIN or your monday.com account) to use it. Donors the office has invited can sign in with their donor code to follow the projects they funded.

    FOR THE FIELD TEAM
    • One map of every project, coloured by how it is doing: working, needs repair or not working.
    • Pin a project's exact location with your phone's GPS in one tap.
    • Report a visit: check in, choose the condition, add readings, photos and a voice note.
    • Works without a signal. Reports wait on the phone and send themselves when you are back online.
    • Save map areas for offline use before you drive out.
    • Plan a route to the sites that are due a visit, with directions in Google Maps.
    • Scan a project's RAFIKI QR sticker to open it and check in.
    • Notifications when a repair is reported or a visit is assigned to you.

    FOR THE OFFICE
    • See open repairs, assign them and follow them until they are fixed.
    • Review reports, costs and photos from the field.
    • Manage the team, PINs and donor access.
    • Optional live team map: people choose to share their location during work hours, and only the latest point is kept, for 12 hours.

    FOR DONORS
    • Follow the projects you funded: photos, visit reports, repairs and thank-you messages from the communities.

    IN ENGLISH AND KISWAHILI
    Switch language at any time in Settings. Light, dark and high-contrast sun themes, and larger text.

    Everything you add goes to RAFIKI Outreach's own project records. No ads and no tracking.

## Kiswahili (sw)

**Jina la programu** (10 of 30)

    RAFIKI Pin

**Maelezo mafupi** (77 of 80)

    Programu ya uwanjani ya RAFIKI Outreach: weka visima, ripoti ziara, fuatilia.

**Maelezo kamili**

    RAFIKI Pin ni programu ya uwanjani ya RAFIKI Outreach, shirika la hisani linalochimba visima na kujenga shule, zahanati na mashamba pamoja na jamii nchini Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda na Malawi.

    Ni chombo cha kazi kwa wafanyakazi na watu wa kujitolea wa RAFIKI Outreach. Unahitaji kuingia kupitia ofisi (PIN yako binafsi au akaunti yako ya monday.com) ili kuitumia. Wafadhili walioalikwa na ofisi wanaweza kuingia kwa msimbo wao wa mfadhili kufuatilia miradi waliyofadhili.

    KWA TIMU YA UWANJANI
    • Ramani moja ya miradi yote, kwa rangi kulingana na hali yake: inafanya kazi, inahitaji matengenezo au haifanyi kazi.
    • Weka mahali hasa pa mradi kwa GPS ya simu yako kwa mguso mmoja.
    • Ripoti ziara: ingia, chagua hali, ongeza vipimo, picha na ujumbe wa sauti.
    • Inafanya kazi bila mtandao. Ripoti zinasubiri kwenye simu na zinatumwa zenyewe ukipata mtandao tena.
    • Hifadhi maeneo ya ramani kwa matumizi bila mtandao kabla ya safari.
    • Panga njia kwenda kwenye miradi inayohitaji ziara, na maelekezo kwenye Google Maps.
    • Changanua kibandiko cha QR cha RAFIKI cha mradi kuufungua na kuingia.
    • Arifa matengenezo yanaporipotiwa au ziara inapokabidhiwa kwako.

    KWA OFISI
    • Ona matengenezo yaliyo wazi, yakabidhi na uyafuatilie hadi yakamilike.
    • Kagua ripoti, gharama na picha kutoka uwanjani.
    • Simamia timu, PIN na ruhusa za wafadhili.
    • Ramani ya timu kwa hiari: kila mtu anachagua kushiriki mahali alipo wakati wa kazi, na ni mahali pa mwisho tu panapohifadhiwa, kwa saa 12.

    KWA WAFADHILI
    • Fuatilia miradi uliyofadhili: picha, ripoti za ziara, matengenezo na ujumbe wa shukrani kutoka kwa jamii.

    KWA KIINGEREZA NA KISWAHILI
    Badilisha lugha wakati wowote kwenye Mipangilio. Mandhari ya mwanga, giza na jua, na maandishi makubwa zaidi.

    Kila unachoongeza kinaenda kwenye kumbukumbu za miradi za RAFIKI Outreach. Hakuna matangazo wala ufuatiliaji.
