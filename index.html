Reaching water

Even though clean water might only be 50 meters beneath their feet, it is not uncommon for men, women, & children to walk upwards of 3 miles to collect and carry water from contaminated sources. Not just once, but multiple times a day.

How we drill

We use a portable, hand crank borehole drill created by the village drill.

It allows drilling in 75% more places at 75% less the cost than a typical drilling rig.

(villagedrill.com)Learn more

Sponsor a well

Schools, businesses, churches and friend groups can fund a whole well together. $4,000 to $5,000 drills a complete borehole for a community.

Every finished well belongs to the community that looks after and maintains it, so the water keeps flowing for years.
