RAFIKI Pin privacy policy

Last updated: 27 Oct 2026.

In short. RAFIKI Pin is the field app of RAFIKI Outreach, a charity that builds and looks after wells, schools and health projects in East Africa. Our staff and volunteers use it to record site visits, and invited donors use it to follow the projects they funded. We collect what the work needs: who you are on the team, where a project is, the reports, photos and voice notes you add, and, only if you switch it on, where you are during work hours. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the RAFIKI Pin app for Android (from Google Play or from our website), the web version at pin.rafikioutreach.org, and donor mode in both. Our public website has its own privacy policy.

RAFIKI Outreach is responsible for the information described here. You can reach us at info@rafikioutreach.org.

Who can use the app

The app is a work tool for adult RAFIKI Outreach staff and volunteers (18 or over), who are given access by the office, and for donors the office invites. It is not meant for children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. There is no public sign-up.

What we collect, and why

Your account

  • Name, and how you sign in. You sign in with a PIN the office gives you or with your monday.com account. We keep your name, your home country, whether you are an office admin, and when you last used the app. PINs are stored on our server only as a scrambled (hashed) value. If you use monday.com, your monday.com sign-in token is kept on our server, encrypted, so the changes you make show your name in monday.com; your phone keeps only a session cookie, which expires after 180 days.
  • Contact details the office adds. The office may add your phone number, e-mail address and role to the team list, so teammates can call or message you from the app. Team members can see the team list. You can ask the office to change or remove them.

Location

  • While you use the app, with your permission, the app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, to sort projects by distance, and to check you in at a site.
  • When you pin a project, report a visit, add a new site or a road report, the location you pin (latitude, longitude and how accurate it was) is sent with it. Project locations are part of our project records.
  • Share my location with the office is off unless you switch it on (Me › Settings › Security and alerts). When it is on, during the work hours you choose, the app sends your position at most every 10 minutes. Only the latest point is kept, for 12 hours. Office admins can see it on the team map, and people on the same trip see where their trip's team is. Switching it off deletes it at once. The app never asks for background location permission.

Photos, voice notes and receipts

  • Photos you take or choose for a visit or a project are sent to our server and attached to the project's notes in monday.com. The app makes them smaller first, which also removes the camera's hidden details (such as the photo's GPS tag). Photos are not published on our website by themselves; the office chooses any that are shown publicly or to a project's donors.
  • Voice notes (up to 2 minutes, with your permission to use the microphone) are attached to the visit note in the same way. To make them searchable, the audio may be sent to a speech-to-text service that types it out; the text is added to the same note.
  • Receipt photos for trip costs are kept with the trip for about 400 days.

Your work in the app

  • Visit reports, notes, repairs, readings, new sites, trip plans and costs. These are the records of our projects. Each one carries your name and the time.
  • Activity. We keep a log of what the team did in the app in the last 30 days, so the team's notifications work.
  • Settings such as language, appearance and offline map areas stay on your phone.

Team chat

  • Messages, photos, videos, voice notes, contacts and locations you send in a chat are stored on our server so the people in that chat can read them. Videos are made smaller on your phone before they are sent. Only members of a chat can read it; donors and the shared team PIN cannot use chat.
  • If you share your live location in a chat, the people in that chat see where you are until it ends or you stop it.
  • You can delete a message for everyone; its content is then removed from the chat.

Trips

  • Trip plans hold the stops, dates, the people going, the vehicle, fuel logs, costs and receipt photos. The places of the stops are sent to route and weather services (see below), without names.
  • During a trip, the app records when each stop was checked in. The office can make a read-only share link for a trip (for example for a driver); it shows the plan, never PINs, the team's phone numbers, costs or private notes.

Donor mode

  • Donors sign in with a private link or a donor code from the office. The office keeps the donor's name, e-mail address, organisation and the projects they supported. The link and code are stored on our server only as hashed values.
  • The app shows those projects' updates, approved photos and thank-you messages. Messages a donor sends to us from the app open in their own e-mail or WhatsApp app.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no crash-reporting service, in the app.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs from your phone. The app reads QR stickers with Google's code scanner, which works on the phone without giving the app camera access.
  • When you report a problem with the app, it opens your e-mail app with the app version and phone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that e-mail.

Where it is stored

  • Our server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages, Workers KV, Durable Objects and R2 storage). Team lists, repairs, the activity log, chats, trip plans, receipts and donor records are kept there.
  • monday.com is where RAFIKI Outreach keeps its project records. Visit notes, photos and voice notes are attached to the project there.
  • Your phone keeps a copy of the project list, your settings and work waiting to be sent, so the app works without a signal. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Everything the app sends to our server, and from our server to these services, travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • Project records in monday.com (visit notes, photos, voice notes and their typed text) are kept as part of the project's history for as long as RAFIKI Outreach looks after the project.
  • Shared live location: 12 hours, or until you switch sharing off.
  • The team activity log: 30 days. Undo information for a pinned location: 90 days.
  • Receipt photos and trip tracks: about 400 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 180 days, or until you sign out.
  • Chat messages: until they are deleted.
  • Team list entries and donor records: until the office removes them.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can switch off location, microphone and notifications at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working; features that need them will ask again.
  • You can switch off Share my location with the office at any time; your shared point is deleted at once.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on the phone.
  • To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, including your team account, contact details or donor record, e-mail info@rafikioutreach.org from the address we know, or ask the office. We will answer within 30 days. We may keep records we need for our projects' history or by law, such as visit reports and costs; when you ask, we remove or replace your name and contact details on what we keep.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed PINs and donor codes, encrypted monday.com tokens, rate limits on sign-in, and an optional fingerprint or PIN lock in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if you think your PIN or phone has been lost, tell the office so it can switch your access off.

Changes

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
